What Is Income Investing?

Most investors wait and hope — hoping their stocks go up, hoping the market cooperates. Income investors don't wait. They put their portfolio to work generating cash — month after month, regardless of what the market does.

Through options strategies like covered calls and cash-secured puts, you can collect real premium income from your existing positions. It's what institutional traders and smart retail investors have done for decades.

The Playbook

Five Strategies. One System.

Each strategy has a specific role in your income portfolio. Learn when to use each one — and how to track every trade.

📞

Covered Calls

Own 100 shares of stock? Sell someone the right to buy it at a higher price. You collect the premium upfront — they take the upside risk. Repeat monthly.

Core Income
🛡️

Cash-Secured Puts

Set your price to buy a stock, then sell a put at that strike. If it falls to your price, you buy at a discount. If it doesn't, you keep the premium. Win either way.

Core Income
📈

LEAPS & Swings

Long-dated options (LEAPS) give you stock-like exposure at a fraction of the cost. Use them for directional plays with defined risk and built-in leverage.

Growth
🎯

Credit Spreads

Put spreads, call spreads, iron condors — sell premium with a defined max loss. These are the high-probability, income-generating workhorses of a professional portfolio.

Core Income
✍️

Buy-Writes

Buy the stock and immediately sell the covered call in one move. Lower your cost basis from day one and start collecting income the moment you enter the position.
