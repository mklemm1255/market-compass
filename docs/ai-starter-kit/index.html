What's Inside

Six Parts, One Workflow

You are not training a new AI model. You are giving an existing assistant a clear, reusable description of your goals, strategies, risk limits, decision rules, and verification standards.

1

Guided setup interview

The AI asks one question at a time and learns how you actually invest — goals, style, approved and prohibited strategies, sizing, entries, exits, and event rules.

2

Portable investing profile

The AI writes a saved profile you can correct, update, and move between platforms when memory resets or you switch tools.

3

Company & trade-plan prompt

A reusable request for research, strategy comparison, management rules, and a defined invalidation point — with grades for company, valuation, and today's setup kept separate.

4

Human verification checklist

A required audit of facts, dates, filings, charts, option chains, events, and personal risk before a single dollar moves.

5

Organization system

A simple structure that keeps research, trade plans, journals, and system builds from turning into a pile of forgotten conversations.

6

Final human authority

The AI can organize and challenge. You verify and decide. A valid result may be a trade, a conditional plan, waiting, or no trade at all.

AI proposes. You decide.

AI IDEA → CURRENT SOURCES → CHART → ACTUAL TRADE → PERSONAL RISK → HUMAN DECISION

A prompt is not a truth serum. Telling an AI not to guess does not make mistakes impossible. Your protection comes from checking sources, dates, numbers, charts, live trade data, and personal risk.
