Inside the Worksheet

Think It Through Before
You Call Your Advisor

A Roth conversion decision has a lot of moving parts. This worksheet helps you organize your thinking — so your next professional conversation is focused, informed, and efficient.

📊

Tax Bracket Snapshot

Map out where you sit today and estimate where a conversion would push your taxable income — before you make any moves.

⏱️

Time Horizon Check

Evaluate how many years you have before RMDs kick in and whether your timeline supports paying taxes now at a potentially lower rate.

🏥

Medicare & IRMAA Awareness

A conversion can push income above IRMAA thresholds, increasing Medicare Part B and D premiums. The worksheet flags this before it surprises you.

💰

Social Security Impact

Higher income in a conversion year can increase how much of your Social Security benefit is taxable. Know the range before you decide.

🧮

Conversion Amount Sizing

Think through what amount to convert — filling up a bracket, partial conversions, or full conversions — and what each means for your tax bill.

📋

Advisor Prep Checklist

Arrive at your CPA or financial advisor meeting with your questions answered and your numbers organized — so the conversation goes straight to decisions.
